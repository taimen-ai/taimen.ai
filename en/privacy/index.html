Privacy policy

Version of 2026-10-03

What this policy covers

This policy describes how the site taimen.ai handles data. The site is the public page of the Taimen project.

What we collect

Only what you send through the pilot request form: your name, work e-mail, company and the description of the work you would like to hand over.

The site does not use analytics, advertising trackers or third-party widgets.

Why we collect it

To reply to your request and discuss a pilot. We do not use these data for mailing lists or advertising.

Where the data go

A request is delivered to the project team as a message (Telegram, e-mail) and is written to the request log on the site's server. Requests are kept for as long as the correspondence on them lasts.

What is stored in your browser

Two settings: the chosen language (the cookie “lang”) and the chosen theme. They contain no personal data and are not used for tracking, so the site shows no consent banner.

Your rights

You can ask what data about you we hold, and ask us to correct or delete them. Write to mail@taimen.ai or to @monthu on Telegram.

Operator

The legal entity operating the project is not yet named here. This section will be completed when it is; until then, use the contacts above for any question about your data.
